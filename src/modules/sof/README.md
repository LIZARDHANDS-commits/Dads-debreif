# SOF Dashboard

The weather screen a Supervisor of Flying keeps open on a desk: the weather now and forecast at home and at the alternates, each report with its age. The spec is [`docs/modules/sof/spec.md`](../../../docs/modules/sof/spec.md); the plan is [`docs/modules/sof/plan.md`](../../../docs/modules/sof/plan.md). This folder holds tasks 1 to 5 so far: the wave, card and caution decisions, and a screen with live weather, the caution banner, the waves and the 24-hour timeline. The map and the rest come in later tasks.

## What the screen shows

- **SOF bar:** the DTG, the weather feed in words and a symbol (`Weather 2 min ago ✓`, `Refreshing…`, `STALE 48 min ⚠`, `Failed, showing 12 min old ⚠`, `Off`), and Refresh. Traffic is a map layer (SOF-7, task 7b), not a link here. The feed status can be focused and says where the weather came from and when it asks again.
- **Airfield cards:** home, then each alternate. Each shows the raw METAR and TAF as text with the report's own time and age, `STALE` in words for an old report (greyed), the words for a missing, cancelled or NIL report, `Last refresh failed` for a report the last round couldn't refresh, and the limit result in words.
- **Every feed failing:** its own row above the cards says so, names which sources failed and when, and says how old the reports still shown are. The cards keep the last reports.
- **Settings:** one closed menu, "SOF settings", holds the alternate trigger (Local (MTCA) 2000/3 by default, or Cross-country 3000/3) and the two limit numbers. A typed limit snaps up to the next 100 ft or quarter mile when committed, so the box shows what the check uses. The banner switch and the lightning radius have defaults (`SETTINGS_DEFAULTS`) but get their controls in tasks 3 and 7. Reset puts every setting back. Stored settings are range-checked on read.

- **Caution banner:** one line per caution with its level in words and a symbol; Acknowledge and Acknowledge all. Marked words inside the report are not built yet (wx exposes no word positions). "Show the new-caution banner" in SOF settings turns it off (on by default); cautions are still worked out and acknowledgements pruned. The lightning hook is `screen.extraCautions`.
- **Waves:** up to 5, home local time with the zone, Today or Tomorrow. Each has a chip; pressing it lists every hit and shows that wave's result on each alternate card. New waves start with blank times.
- **24-hour timeline:** a row per airfield, Zulu axis first (Local first follows the app setting) with a local row, NATO-state pieces labelled in words, hatched when below limits, wave bands with landing and +1 h marks, the METAR mark and the now line. Hover or focus shows the card in words. Open by default; the choice is kept under `timelineCollapsed`.
- **3D view (SOF-39, phase 1):** the **3D** button in the map controls swaps the map area for a 3D picture of the 450 NM square round home (it was 250; Dad, 7 Oct) (it reads **2D** while shown, and the ADS-B Exchange view and 3D never show together). Satellite ground, radar and lightning on it with the same stale fading, home and alternates as pins with their category in words, each METAR's cloud layers as flat decks 10 NM across, the 25 and 50 NM rings, and "Heights ×5" in the corner. Drag to turn, wheel or pinch to zoom, Home (or the Home key) resets. A pin is a button that shows its card's result line, and the camera never moves on a click. An airfield outside the square is named, not drawn. No WebGL: the button stays and says why on hover. Nothing in it raises or clears a caution.
- **Live aircraft in 3D (SOF-39 phase 4, SOF-40):** with the traffic relay's address set, the same **Traffic** switch that drives the 2D layer also shows the relay's aircraft in 3D (one feed, the 2D layer's own checks, the same stale fade and the same pause for a hidden tab). Each stands at its position and pressure altitude (times the height scale; "ground" is the field's elevation) pointing along its track (degrees true; the 3D world is north up). A T-6 (type `TEX2`) is the CT-156 model drawn large, with a cyan halo, a drop line to the ground and its tag always on ("TEX21 5,500": callsign or registration or hex, then hundreds of feet, or FL from 18,000 ft). Other aircraft are small grey stand-ins whose tag shows on hover, on `]` and `[` (step through them, as in 2D) or all the time when the Labels choice is on; military ones are amber with a ring. Clicking an aircraft or its tag shows the 2D hover facts in a small tag, and never moves the camera. An aircraft with no height is not drawn (the line says how many), and over 150 only the nearest are drawn (T-6s first). If the relay fails the aircraft fade and go as in 2D, and the line says "Traffic unavailable, last good 1842Z". The credit "Aircraft: adsb.lol (ODbL 1.0)" shows while the layer is on.
- **Airspace and the TACNAV routes in 3D (SOF-39, phase 3):** two toggles under the model controls, **Airspace** and **TACNAV** (both on, `aria-pressed`), and a closed "Airspace key". Each airspace entry in `airspace-data.js` is a see-through prism from its floor to its ceiling (about 8 % fill, `AIRSPACE_FILL_OPACITY`, an estimate; drawn without depth writing so clouds and aircraft read through it) with a thin outline on its top and bottom rings and its corners. Edge colour by kind, also said in words in the key: restricted or danger areas red (danger is red only), advisory areas amber, terminal airspace blue, control zones light blue, MTCA cyan, other grey. Each has a label at the middle of its top, "CYR303 SFC–FL180": a restricted area's label always shows its limits, the others show the id and open to the limits on hover or focus (they are buttons), and the hover text gives the kind, class and source. SFC is the ground at home's elevation; AGL is home's elevation plus the height (ground taken as flat prairie, an estimate); FL is read as feet above sea level (pressure altitude taken as altitude, an approximation); UNL is drawn up to 60,000 ft; all times the height scale. The routes of the Debrief's `ROUTES` with TAC in the name (TAC NAV 2, TAC NAV 3, TACNAV 1, TACNAV 4, TAC test North and South) are dashed lines at home's elevation plus 500 ft, named at their first point, with the note "500 ft AGL, ground taken as flat at CYMJ's elevation (estimate)" in the key. While `AIRSPACE` is empty the Airspace toggle reads "Airspace (no data yet)" and is disabled with the reason on hover. An entry that fails `checkAirspace` is skipped and named in the key, never drawn wrong. Nothing in it raises or clears a caution, and it is not a chart or for navigation.
- **Full screen, Orbit and the Airspace log in 3D (SOF-39, Dad 7 Oct):** at the top right of the 3D view, beside "Heights ×5": **Orbit** (the "on" look with `aria-pressed`, off to begin with) turns the camera slowly round home at the zoom and tilt it has, one turn in `ORBIT_SECONDS_PER_TURN` (120 s, an estimate, in `view3d.js`), on the scheduler's frame: the one case where the view draws every frame, and the loop ends the moment Orbit is off, the view is hidden or the module closes. A drag, pinch, the wheel, an arrow key, + or -, Home (key or the bar's button) or the zoom buttons turn it off so the SOF can take over. With reduced motion (the Settings choice or the computer's) it steps `ORBIT_STEP` (15° every 2 s) instead of turning smoothly. **Tour** (beside Orbit, off to begin with) turns Orbit on and flies the camera round the targets in `TOUR_TARGETS` (`tour-model.js`): the Moose Jaw circuit (CYMJ, about 5 NM round), Regina (CYQR, 5 NM), then each airborne T-6 (TEX2) in turn, followed as it moves, about 3 NM round with its tag outlined, about 20 s at each with a smooth fly of about 2 s (a step with reduced motion), then back to the start; a T-6 that leaves the feed is skipped. A caption in the corner says what is shown and "next in 12 s". Any drag, the wheel, a key, Home or the zoom buttons stops the Tour and Orbit; it runs in Orbit's own frame loop. **Full screen** (also on the 2D map's bar since 7 Oct) puts the whole SOF picture over the window: the map (2D or 3D), the airfield column and the timeline strip, with a fixed CSS layer if the browser refuses; the label flips to **Exit full screen**, Escape exits, and the picture is drawn again at the new size. Hovering an airfield row there shows its card, as it does outside full screen. The **Airspace log** panel (bottom right, only while the Traffic layer is on) lists the aircraft that are not T-6s (relay type other than `TEX2`, or none) inside CYA304, CYA305 or CYA307 (`WATCHED_AREAS`, `airspace-occupancy.js`) now, each in amber with ⚠ and words ("0412Z  ⚠ WJA1453 (B737) in CYA305 at 12,000 ft", the time being when it was first seen inside), then the session's entries and exits; its two ticks add T-6 movements and every other airspace volume (only movements after the tick is on are logged). Each such aircraft's tag in 3D is amber with ⚠ and stays on. The count shows on the 2D status strip too ("1 non-T-6 in CYA304/305/307 ⚠"). The log reads every aircraft the relay gave (Military only does not hide any from it), once for each new traffic answer, in 2D as well as 3D. It is information only: it raises no caution and the SOF banner never reads it.
- **Model clouds, winds aloft and the freezing level (SOF-39, phase 2):** while the 3D view is shown it asks Open-Meteo's GEM forecast (free, no key, CC BY 4.0) for a 9 × 9 grid over the square, once on opening and then once an hour. Each cloud level (1000 to 300 hPa, at its mean height) becomes one see-through sheet across the square, like ForeFlight's cloud-cover maps: the 9 × 9 cover is smoothed over it, clear at 30 % or less and white to grey, up to about 85 % opaque at full cover (a level under the ground has no sheet); wind barbs (pennant 50 kt, feather 10, half 5; °M, kt) stand at 850, 700 and 500 hPa at every other grid point; one faint sheet marks the mean freezing level. Low, Mid, High, Winds and Freezing level are toggle buttons (all on), a "Layer" picker shows All the sheets or one level at a time ("850 hPa ≈ 4,900 ft"), and a slider moves the model layers from Now to +24 h (Zulu, with home local beside it); the METAR decks, radar and lightning stay at now, and the view says so. A closed "Model key" explains it and the winds over home, and "Model clouds and winds: Open-Meteo, ECCC GEM (model estimate)" stays on while the layers show. A failed refresh keeps the last good answer on screen until it is 3 hours old, saying "Model refresh failed at 1030Z, showing the 0900Z answer (90 min old)"; past 3 hours (or with no answer yet) the layers are taken away (never frozen) and the panel says "Model clouds unavailable, showing METAR decks only" with the time of the last good answer (or "not yet"). It asks again after 10 minutes. A reply that fails its checks counts as a failed refresh. It is a model estimate and never raises or clears a caution.

## Changing something

- **The 3D height scale:** "3D height scale" in SOF settings (`heightScale3d`, 5 by default, 1 to 20; an estimate for readability, SOF-39). The view's corner says the number in use.
- **How solid a cloud deck is:** `COVER_OPACITY` in `scene3d-model.js` (FEW 25 %, SCT 45 %, BKN 70 %, OVC 90 %; estimates, SOF-39). The deck width is `DECK_NM` (10) and the square is `AREA_NM` (250).
- **How the airspace looks, and how to add an entry:** `AIRSPACE_FILL_OPACITY` (0.08), `TACNAV_AGL_FT` (500), `VIEW_TOP_FT` (60,000) and `CIRCLE_SIDES` (64) in `airspace-model.js`; the edge colours (`KIND_COLOURS`) and the route dashes in `airspace3d.js` (estimates for readability, SOF-39). To add an airspace, write one entry in the shape at the top of `airspace-data.js`, with its `source`; the view checks it and says in its key if it skips it.
- **How the 3D aircraft look:** `SIZE_PX` (a T-6 44 px long on the screen, others 22, a dot 9) and `COLOURS` in `traffic3d.js` (estimates for readability, SOF-39); `MAX_3D_AIRCRAFT` (150) and `T6_TYPE` (`TEX2`) in `scene3d-model.js`; the aircraft's lighting is in `build()` in `view3d.js`.
- **The model layers' numbers** (`model-clouds.js`): the grid (`GRID_SIZE` 13, so 37.5 NM apart), the two requests (`HRDPS_PROFILE`, 12 levels from ECCC's 2.5 km model, and `GEM_PROFILE`, the global model's 8 levels: HRDPS is asked first and is slow, about 40 s, so the global answer shows first and HRDPS replaces it; the levels HRDPS answered on 7 Oct 2026 are in `HRDPS_TRIED_HPA`), the variables and levels (`HOURLY_VARIABLES`, `CLOUD_LEVELS_HPA`, `WIND_LEVELS_HPA`), the cloud line (`CLOUD_COVER_THRESHOLD_PCT`, 30 %; estimate, SOF-39), the low, mid and high stages (`CLOUD_STAGES_FT_AGL`: 6,500 and 20,000 ft above the ground), how long an answer is kept (`STALE_MS`, 3 hours), how often it is asked for (`REFRESH_MS`, 1 hour) and the retry after a failure (`RETRY_MS`, 10 minutes; an estimate). The cloud sheets' look is also in `model-clouds.js`: `CLOUD_SHEET_PX` (256), `CLOUD_SHEET_ALPHA_MAX` (0.85), `CLOUD_SHEET_BLUR_PX`, `CLOUD_SHEET_COLOURS` and the curve `sheetAlpha` (estimates, SOF-39). How the barbs and the freezing sheet look is in `model-layers3d.js`.
- **How sharp the 3D ground is:** `OUTER` and `INNER` in `ground3d.js` (the whole square at Esri zoom 7 on a 1,024 px canvas; a patch `INNER_NM` (120) square at zoom 9 on a 1,536 px canvas; about 36 tiles each, `MAX_GROUND_TILES` 100 the most; estimates).
- **The weather layers in 3D:** the estimates are named in `weather3d-model.js` (`RADAR_DEFAULT_AGL_FT` 8,000, `LIGHTNING_DEFAULT_AGL_FT` 25,000, `SATELLITE_DEFAULT_FT` 30,000, `FRONT_WALL_FT` 6,000, `FLOW_FT_PER_S_PER_KT` 600, `FLOW_PARTICLES` 300) and every one is said in the view's "Weather key".
- **The tour:** `TOUR_TARGETS` (add a target here: a `field` by ICAO or an `aircraft` kind), `TOUR_DWELL_S` (20), `TOUR_FLY_S` (2) and `TOUR_PITCH_DEG` in `tour-model.js`.
- **How often the weather is asked for:** `REFRESH_MS` in `weather.js` (5 minutes, D67). `wake()` refreshes at once when the tab comes back and a round is due.
- **When the weather feed reads STALE:** `STALE_FEED_MIN` in `screen-model.js` (15 minutes, three missed rounds). A single report's own staleness (75 minutes for a METAR, a TAF past its end) is wx's, through `cards.js`.
- **How long a kept report is still shown:** `MAX_KEEP_MS` in `reports-store.js` (3 days). It shows with its age and STALE.
- **A limit's step:** `RANGES` in `settings-model.js`; limits always snap up, never down.
- **A default setting:** `SETTINGS_DEFAULTS` in `settings-model.js`. The trigger presets themselves are wx's (`HOME_TRIGGERS`).
- **The credits line:** `CREDITS` in `screen-model.js`. Add each new source here as its layer lands.
- **The card's layout:** `children()` in `cards-view.js`, and the classes in `sof.css`.

## Things to know

- **What's kept in the browser** (through `app.storage`, scope `sof`): the settings, the last good reports, the wave plan (`plan`), the acknowledged cautions (`cautionAcks`) and whether the timeline is closed (`timelineCollapsed`). If the browser blocks storage they last for the visit only.
- **The 3D view asks for its own pictures** (`feeds3d` in `map.js`): radar, lightning and the GOES cloud picture over the whole 450 NM square, only while 3D is shown (the 2D map's pictures cover only what the 2D view last asked for). They lay on the 3D ground (each only when its 2D layer is on) and, read on a grid, make the radar shafts, the lightning bolts and the satellite sheet. The backup radar (RainViewer tiles) is not drawn in 3D.
- **The model feed** (`createModelFeed` in `model-clouds.js`, started by `map.js` when 3D opens and stopped when it closes) asks through `map-fetch.js` (30 s for the global model, 180 s for HRDPS, 3 and 4 MB caps, no cookies, no redirects) and keeps the last good answer in memory only. Its reply is checked field by field in one function (`checkModelReply`: 169 points, the same times and array lengths for every variable, every number finite and in range); one wrong value fails the whole reply. A `null` is no data for that one value (cloud cover null is not cloudy, a null height drops that level, a null wind draws no barb, a null freezing level is left out of the mean), but nulls in more than half of all the values fail the reply too ("Model data incomplete"). Wind direction is shown in °M with 9° East variation (Patrick, TR-65); `MAG_VARIATION_DEG_E` in `model-clouds.js` repeats Traffic's number because modules do not import each other and there is no shared copy yet.
- **Timers** come only from the module's scheduler scope: the refresh's 5-minute timer (via wx's `startRefresh`) and one 15-second tick that redraws ages and the DTG. Requests are made with an abort signal that unmount fires.
- **Chrome logs a 404 as a console error.** Datamask answers 404 for a station it doesn't have, and that is logged by the browser, not by this code. It only happens when MET Norway has no report for a station, which is also when the card says `No METAR from MET Norway or Datamask`.
