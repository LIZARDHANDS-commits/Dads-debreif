# Run-in Simulator (code)

An orbit, a run-in, a release (or a flypast) and an escape, worked out and flown over the real ground (plan Steps 2 to 5 and 4b, V2.502); and, **proposed and built ahead of Patrick's yes (Dad, 17 Oct 2026: "Build anyway")**, the air refuelling point-parallel rendezvous and join (plan Step 7, RS-15 to RS-18, V2.503). What it does and why: `docs/modules/run-in-sim/` (start with its README and `spec.md`). Training only: inert loads, generic shapes.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.js` | The one screen (the refuelling run worked out by `refuel.js` instead of `pass.js`, with its own marks, card and two cameras shown only on it): Setup, the 3D | 2D switch, the 3D view with the plan view and the map (or the 2D view), the bar (time line with the legs marked, play and pause, ×1 to ×8, the five cameras) and the result cards. "Fly the pass" works the whole pass out first, then playback only reads the stored timeline. |
| `pass.js` | The engine, plain values: the plan (the run-in line, the IP, the orbit) and the numbers worked out to hit (RS-9: the release point with core `carp`, the aim-off for the wind, the roll-in height and speed for a dive, the orbit exit for the time over target), the flight stepped at 0.05 s, the load's fall (core `flyDrop`) and the result with its flags (RS-6). The holding pattern and the figure 8 (RS-11): the pattern built on the IP from `ground-path.js`, held by the pilot's L1 follower, left at the IP heading inbound, and the time over target made by the last lap (`solvePattern`: the last outbound leg stretched or shortened, or the last loop flown between 15° and 45° of bank; `flyPattern` nudges it on the flown arrival). Over the bridge the load stops only where it meets the deck from above or its edge, else falls past it to the ground (RS-12, `targets.js` `deckHit` and `deckMiss`). Its one test: `tests/unit/run-in-sim/pass.test.js`. |
| `refuel.js` | The air refuelling run's engine (RS-15 to RS-18; proposed, built ahead of Patrick's yes), plain values: the KC-130J moved along its anchor (the holding pattern's racetrack from `ground-path.js` `holdLap`) at a set height and true airspeed, its bank drawn from its turn over the ground (core `bankDegFromTurnRate`): a picture with no performance set (RS-Q1); the point-parallel worked out to hit (the offset of two turn radii, the receiver's speed as the tanker's ground speed plus the overtake turned to KIAS at its height, its start found by flying it, the tanker's turn moment and range, or either set by hand); the CT-156 stand-in receiver flown by `pilot.js` on core's point mass to pre-contact behind the left basket (`DROGUE`, `JOIN`, every number an estimate); and the card's numbers and flags (core `closureKt`). Reuses `pass.js`'s `speedAlong`, `windWords` and `climbToward`. Its one test: `tests/unit/run-in-sim/refuel.test.js`. |
| `kc130j.js` | The KC-130J for the refuelling run: the shared C-130J model (`src/ui-kit/c130j-model.js`) with its two wing pods, hoses and baskets as simple shapes from `refuel.js`'s `DROGUE` numbers. A picture only. |
| `pilot.js` | The pilot, built only from core pieces on core's point mass (RS-4): the lift he wants (`dampedClimbG` up and down, `liftTowardAim` as an L1 path follower side to side, on a line, the circle or the pattern's path), turned into G and bank (`gAndBankForLift`), flown inside the roll limit, the G onset and the stall line, with the throttle on the speed. Every gain an estimate (`PILOT`). |
| `aircraft.js` | The aircraft a pass can fly with, each one core's performance set in the shape the engine reads. Today the CT-156 on the T-6A set. |
| `defaults.js` | The Run list (with the refuelling run, proposed), the settings and their defaults (the refuelling run's `AAR_DEFAULTS`, estimates) (the spec's table, all estimates, RS-Q2), the box ranges, and the settings turned into the engine's setup (magnetic to true). |
| `targets.js` | The five generic shapes and their sizes (estimates), each at its own heading (RS-12); their footprints for the plan, their cut along a line for the profile, and the bridge's deck as a slab a load meets (`deckHit`) or misses (`deckMiss`, read long, short, left or right of the run-in). |
| `ground-path.js` | A path over the ground of straight legs and arcs (RS-11): one lap of the holding pattern, one loop of the figure 8, the point a distance along it and the nearest point to the aircraft (looked for round where he was, since the figure 8 crosses itself at the IP). Geometry only. |
| `area.js` | The map projection round the chosen place and the ground squares the terrain and the satellite picture are drawn on. |
| `world3d.js` | The 3D view (for the refuelling run: no target, the anchor and the receiver's track, the KC-130J beside the CT-156, both trails, and the From the tanker and Behind the drogue cameras): the shared terrain with the satellite picture draped on it, the sky, the target shape at its own heading, the planned lines (the pattern or circle from `pass.js` `orbitOutline`), the CT-156 (and its cockpit for the Cockpit camera), the trails and the impact mark. |
| `plan-view.js` | The small plan view (2D) in the 3D view's corner, north up, with the wind and a scale; a click opens the map. The refuelling run: the anchor, the receiver's track and both flown tracks. |
| `view2d.js` | The 2D view behind the 3D | 2D switch (RS-13; for the refuelling run both tracks on the plan, and the profile along the refuelling track or behind the tanker): the plan on the satellite map at true scale (pan and zoom, Fit), and the profile along the run-in through the aim point (At the target, Release to impact, Whole run-in), both following the playback. |
| `map-picker.js` | "Tap the map": the satellite map to tap a place on, with typed latitude and longitude under More. |
| `setup-panel.js` | The Setup column (the refuelling run's Rendezvous time and its Tanker and anchor, Rendezvous and Join boxes in place of the run-in's): the boxes, their one-line summaries, the greyed "set to hit" numbers and the More switches that set them by hand. |
| `card.js` | The result card's words; `refuelCardFor` the refuelling run's (the trail at the roll-out against the plan, the join, the flags). |
| `run-in-sim.css` | The screen's layout and look (tokens from `src/ui-kit/tokens.css`). |

**The cargo offload** is left off the Run list for now: its aircraft, the C-130J, has no performance set yet (RS-Q1, waiting on Patrick). The engine is ready for it: adding it means one entry in `aircraft.js` (the C-130J's set, in the same shape as the CT-156's) and one run in `defaults.js` (`{ id: 'cargo', aircraft: 'c130j', load: 'a22g12' }`), with the C-130J model (`src/ui-kit/c130j-model.js`) drawn in `world3d.js`.

Shared code it uses, never copies: core `point-mass.js`, `flight-math.js`, `t6-performance.js`, `drop.js`, `wind.js`, `atmosphere.js`, `angles.js`, `geo.js`, `units.js`, `terrain-grid.js`; ui-kit `terrain3d.js`, `draped-ground.js`, `sky-clouds.js`, `three-aircraft.js`, `ct156-model.js`, `ct156-cockpit.js`, `map-tiles.js`, `canvas-view.js`, `camera-bar.js`, `panel.js`, `controls.js`, `airfield3d.js` (CYMJ's height), `dom.js`. From other modules (rule book: a module may import another's file when moving it isn't worth it): the Debrief's typed-wind blend (`debrief/drop.js` `dropWind`), Moose Jaw's magnetic variation (`traffic/airfield.js`, TR-65) and the one version label (`traffic/version.js`).
