# EP Tabletop (code)

The emergency procedures tabletop: the one-screen version (plan Step 1), the instructor's phone by QR code (plan Step 2), the systems model, where causes drive the gauges and the gauges drive the lights (plan Step 3, EP-11), the picture cues (Step 4, EP-12) and the aircraft flying the picks on the shared point mass (Step 5, EP-13, built on EP-Q5's working answer (a) while it stays open; not yet seen). What it does and why: `docs/modules/ep-tabletop/` (start with its README, `spec.md` and `systems-plan.md`).

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.js` | Picks the screen from the address: the phone's console (the QR code's address) or the student's screen. Holds the version label and the module's settings. |
| `screen.js` | The student's screen: the student's side (3D cockpit, situation, A-A-B-C-D-E-F step track, "Time stands still", what has been revealed), the Instructor switch, the QR code and the link. Runs the clock on the shell's scheduler. |
| `phone.js` | The instructor's phone: the console, the scenario running there, sending only the student view. |
| `student-view.js` | `studentView(session)`, the only thing the student's side is drawn from and the only thing the phone sends (EP-9), and `cleanStudentView(raw)`, which checks and clamps one arriving over the link. Never carries a cause, its severity or its trend. Its one test: `tests/unit/ep-tabletop/student-view.test.js`. |
| `link.js` | The phone link behind one interface, `createLink`: the relay mailbox (`relay/ep-link.js`, EP-8), the QR code's address, the session id and key. |
| `scenario.js` | The scenario engine, pure: clock, steps, causes (`faults`) and typed values (`overrides`), developments by time, step or tap (each may set indications, a cause or a change to one), reveal-on-ask, pending edits and cause changes applied on Apply, Now/Later/Add, prompts, marks, notes, Declare boxes, log; the flight (`flight`, stepped inside `advance`, to each timed development's own time first) and the `pick` action (flies at once; Declare goes to D). A typed altitude, airspeed or heading puts the aircraft there (`placeFlight`). No flight maths of its own. Its one test: `tests/unit/ep-tabletop/scenario.test.js`. |
| `flight.js` | The aircraft (EP-13, `systems-plan.md` 4.2): a point mass flown by G and bank on core `stepPointMass` at a fixed 0.05 s step, deterministic. True airspeed in the model, indicated on the gauge. Thrust and drag from core `excessFnFor` by the engine state the causes leave (`engineOf`: healthy, failed and feathered, rough). Roll and G onset inside `rollWithinT6A` and `T6A_G_ONSET`; the wing's G (`stallLimitG`) always holds; VMO and 7 G flagged (`flagsOf`). Wind added for the ground track; headings magnetic, 9° E (`MAG_VARIATION_DEG_E`, the module's one copy). The fields round Moose Jaw and ground contact at the nearest field's elevation: "landed" on a runway, else "touched down off the runway" with where. The glide ring (`glideRingOf`) is Traffic's `calculateGlideFootprint`, imported. Its one test: `tests/unit/ep-tabletop/flight.test.js`. |
| `manoeuvres.js` | The student's picks as data (`MANOEUVRES`: id, words, the fields each needs, its start) and the small pilots that fly them: a lateral half (straight, a turn to a heading, toward a field, an orbit) asking for bank, and a vertical half (level, climb, descend, zoom, glide, slow down, speed up) asking for G and throttle. `cleanPick` checks a pick; `pickWords` says it. Plain words only (EP-3). The nearest suitable field from a working list until Patrick names his (EP-Q6). |
| `systems.js` | The systems model, pure (EP-11): `gaugesOf` (the healthy start with each cause's effect by the clock), `lightsOf` (lights from thresholds; MASTER CAUTION with any caution, MASTER WARN with FIRE), `shownOf` (typed values win, then lights, then gauges). `FAULT_EFFECTS` and `LIGHT_THRESHOLDS` hold every rate and threshold with its source; all are estimates for now (EP-Q10). Its one test: `tests/unit/ep-tabletop/systems.test.js`. |
| `scenarios.js` | The example scenarios, "Engine roughness in the area" and "Oil pressure dropping on departure" (indications and causes only; each number sourced or marked an estimate; never checklist or boldface text, EP-3). |
| `indications.js` | What can be set and asked about (engine numbers, lights, words for outside cues, gear and flaps, flight instruments), their words and units, and the panel turned into the shared cockpit's numbers. |
| `console.js` | The instructor's console frame, on the phone (one column, big buttons) or behind the one-screen Instructor switch: the header, the scenario picker (before Start), the Apply bar, and the sections below put together. |
| `console-time.js` | Console: Time (clock, Start/Pause, back to A, re-analyse, next step, step buttons) and Next development (Now/Later/+Add). |
| `console-faults.js` | Console: Faults (cause picker, severity slider, Worsening/Steady/Improving, Clear; each cause's effect with "(estimate)"). |
| `console-picks.js` | Console: Pick for the student (plan Step 5): a pick from the list with its heading or field, flown at once; what the aircraft is flying, its numbers, the engine, the fields inside the glide ring, any published limit flagged, the outcome. |
| `console-indications.js` | Console: Indications (every indication with Reveal and its edit, "typed" when the instructor's value wins, a threshold and "(estimate)" beside a light or gauge that follows one). |
| `console-cues.js` | Console: Cues (prompt smoke, fire glow or roughness; haze, glow and shake levels; flicker a light). Set like any indication: they wait for Apply. |
| `console-marks.js` | Console: Prompts and marks (FEVER and TCCC, marks and notes) and Declare. |
| `world3d.js` | The 3D view: the shared CT-156 cockpit (live engine displays, `liveEngine`) at the front-seat eye over Moose Jaw (shared terrain, satellite picture, runways, sky). Drag to look round, double-click to look ahead, Shift to zoom. |
| `ep-tabletop.css` | The screen's layout and look (tokens from `src/ui-kit/tokens.css`). |

Shared code it uses, never copies: `src/ui-kit/qr.js`, `src/wx/relay.js`, `src/storage/settings.js`, `src/ui-kit/ct156-cockpit.js` and `ct156-model.js`, `terrain3d.js`, `draped-ground.js`, `airfield3d.js`, `sky-clouds.js`, `three-aircraft.js`, `dom.js`; `src/airfields/cymj-runways.js`, `airports-data.js`; `src/core/geo.js`, `terrain-grid.js`, `angles.js`, `units.js`, `point-mass.js`, `t6-performance.js`, `flight-math.js`, `wind.js`. One other module's file, imported and never edited: Traffic's `src/modules/traffic/pfl-geometry.js` for the glide ring (`calculateGlideFootprint`) and the PFL numbers the picks fly (`PFL`: glide speeds 125 / 120, the zoom's 2 G, 20°, 145 and 150 KIAS, the 30° orbit, the stall by configuration). Moving the glide ring to core is the plan's later preference (`systems-plan.md` 4.3).

**Picture cues (EP-12, plan Step 4).** A cause or the instructor sets `fx.haze`, `fx.glow` (0 to 1), `fx.shakeDeg` (degrees) and `fx.flicker.<light>`; `student-view.js` `cuesOf` carries them as `cues` once applied (a flickering light only once revealed), and `cleanStudentView` clamps them. `screen.js` draws the haze and glow as CSS overlays, flickers lit lights by the clock (`indications.js` `flickerDark`, at most 2 a second, with no change to the shared cockpit) and passes the swinging shake to `world3d.setPose`. Flicker and shake stop while time stands still. Numbers, all picture choices (not flying numbers; no source beyond the plan's table): flicker 2 on-off cycles a second; shake 4 swings a second, 0.3 degrees at severity 1 (cap 1 degree); fire glow 0.7 and haze 0.35; smoke haze 0.8 at severity 1; the overlay colours. 3D cues are on `future.md`.

**The flight (EP-13, plan Step 5).** Built on EP-Q5's working answer (a); EP-Q5 is still open with Patrick. Each step the lateral pilot asks for a bank, the vertical pilot for the G that holds its path and a throttle; `flight.js` eases them in under the aircraft's roll and G onset and caps the G at the wing's. In one-screen mode the session flies on the screen; in phone mode the phone flies it and the student's screen carries its last state on by dead reckoning between sends (`student-view.js` `flightAhead`), never running the model there (it would need the engine's state, a cause). The outside view's bank sign was fixed in this step (`world3d.js` `setPose`: a right turn had drawn the world banked left, unseen while the bank was always level).
Numbers and their sources (estimates are labelled so in the code and on the console): turn bank 30° (estimate); the pilot's roll 45°/s and 90°/s² (Traffic's estimates) under the T-6A's; heading roll-out 2.5° of bank per degree to go (estimate); climb and descend on an 8° path (estimate) for 2,000 ft (the Formation sim's estimate), never levelling below 1,000 ft above the ground (estimate); climb never slower than 140 KIAS (best-rate climb, SMM 3.14 para 35 and EFIG p.126 as cited in core); slow down to 150 and speed up to 230 KIAS (estimates); height closed over 10 s at most 2,000 ft/min, speed on the nose 0.5° per knot up to 15°, on the throttle over 5 s at most 3 kt/s, nose smoothness 0.5 rad/s (pilot estimates, the last as the Formation sim's); best glide 125 KIAS clean and 120 gear down (Patrick's C1, EP-Q11's working answer; the reference data in core `T6A_GLIDE` say 125 and 105); the zoom's 2 G to 20°, through 145, onto 125, only above 150 KIAS (reference data; Patrick's C7); orbit 30° (SMM 13.5 para 11); the pilot never slower than the stall plus the shaker's 7 kt (core `T6A_MANOEUVRE`, reference); the pilot's G 0 to 2 (the reference zoom's push; the glide's 2 G, TR-51); stall 86 KIAS clean, gear and take-off flap (Patrick, 30 Sep), 76 landing flap (Traffic's `PFL.stallKias`, source to confirm); rough engine thrust loss 40 % at severity 1, 20 % at the default 0.5 (estimate, Patrick's ruling wanted); flaps with the gear up flown on the flap row, which has the gear down (estimate). The failed engine takes the thrust at once; the gauges' 10 s run-down is the gauges' picture.
Not built yet: High Key and the forced-landing pattern (a later step); a round-out and flare at touchdown (the touchdown is where the path meets the field's elevation; the 3D terrain is a picture only); the throttle moving the torque and fuel flow gauges; gear and flap travel time and their limit speeds.
