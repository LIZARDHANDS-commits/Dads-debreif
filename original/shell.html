ABOUT THE PROJECT

ABOUT DAD

FLY • LEARN • DEBRIEF • IMPROVE

I'm a U.S. Air Force exchange officer in Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan. I built DAD'S Aviators Webtool Suite to help aviators visualize complex problems, make informed decisions, and improve the learning and debriefing process.

These tools are built around a simple idea: make useful aviation concepts easier to see, understand, discuss, and learn from.

AI was leveraged to assist in the development of these tools.

Turn Setup

Delayed turns can initiate by time delay, clock cue, or auto equal-track timing. Offset Box: front element initiates first, far side first; #3 solves to the center slot; #4 solves by ground track to the inner or outer aft side. Hook: all turn together. Shackle/cross: elements turn opposite directions.
