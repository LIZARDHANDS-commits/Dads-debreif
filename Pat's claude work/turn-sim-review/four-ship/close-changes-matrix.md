# Four-ship close changes: what the SMM says against what we script

Read-only research, 6 Oct 2026, for Patrick. Nothing in the repo was edited. Manual text is quoted or closely paraphrased here, so this file stays in the project files and never goes in the repo.

**Summary**
1. 28 rows: 24 are changes between the close formations (finger, echelon, box, line astern, route), 4 are the adjacent changes to and from fighting wing.
2. The SMM or the AFM7 brief actually describes 8 of the 24 close rows: 4 match (A1, A5, B1, C2), 2 partly (B3, C1), 2 do not (A2, A3). The other 16 close rows the manuals do not describe at all; our code chains the described moves (mostly through finger), and A4 and B2 follow a reasonable inference.
3. Biggest difference, Patrick's rule: when #3 and #4 cross under Lead (finger to echelon on #2's side, echelon to finger, and both halves of echelon to echelon) #4 flies 45-50 ft outboard of #3 and about 60 ft behind, not in line behind #3. They cross only 1.2 s apart and #4 moves sideways at the same rate as #3, not faster. #4 gets into the column behind #3 only after #3 is across. The AFM7 p.19 picture shows a column crossing together.
4. Echelon to echelon on the other side is flown as two separate changes through finger: #2 crosses alone, all four sit still for about 10 s, then #3 and #4 cross, 90 s in all. Box to the other box side is a 4-change chain of 166 s, and "box to the other finger" 130 s; the SMM only says to reform in the same finger (para 91).
5. Everywhere, the next step starts only when the whole last step has settled (5-8 s of dead time each), where the SMM says "when #2 sees room" (para 87). Route moves all three wingmen at once; the briefs give an order (#2, #3, #4) only for the rejoin from fighting wing.

**How I checked.** SMM 16.32 paras 85-93 and 12.20 paras 44-47 read in `smm.txt`; Fig 16.27 and AFM7 pp.18-20 pictures looked at; the ratified table (`moves-from-the-manuals.md` sections 2, 7, 8); the code read in `four-plan.js`, `four-close.js`, `four-legs.js`, `slots.js`, `transitions.js`, `rates.js`. Then a dry run with `createFormation({ ships: 4, wingSide: 'right' })` (the default start: Lead 200 KIAS in finger, #2 on the right, so "finger left", #3 and #4 on the left), pressed through fighting wing then finger, Instructor rates (default; close-in closure 24 KIAS, 40 ft/s), logged every 0.05 s against Lead. Mirror starts (#2 on the left) were run for the echelon-left and line-astern rows and behave the same.

**Words.** "Finger left" = #3 and #4 on Lead's left, #2 on the right (SMM Fig 16.27). "Echelon right" = all three on Lead's right. "Crossing" = passing under Lead's flight path. Positions are feet against Lead (back, out, below); all speeds are KIAS. Heights, feet and times are our code's estimates unless a source is given. Min gap is the smallest centre-to-centre distance between any two aircraft in the dry run.
