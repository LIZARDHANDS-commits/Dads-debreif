# Moose Jaw routes in true feet: draft point list (5 Oct 2026)

Draft for Patrick's check, not built. Traffic thread. Picture: [draft-29L-pattern.jpg](draft-29L-pattern.jpg) (Esri photo, true scale, north up).

**How it was made.** EFIG Fig 3-10 (p.211) was laid over Esri's true-scale photo, using the 29L runway and the figure's ground references. Race Track Lake and the Sukanen Ship intersection land within about 60 ft of the figure, and Snowdys Springs within about 300 ft. The figure's photo is squashed top to bottom (58 ft per pixel along the runway, 67 across), which the fit allows for. Its lines are hand-drawn, so treat a leg's ends as good to about ±300 ft. The ground references are better than that.

**Frame.** Feet east and north of the field reference point (50.3303 N, 105.5592 W; CAP aerodrome chart ARP N50 19.82 W105 33.55). "Along" is NM from the 29L threshold toward the approach end (118.6° true). "Out" is NM south of the 29L centreline. Heights are MSL; speeds are KIAS.

## Rejoins (Patrick 01:05Z: the rejoin lines define the base leg; SMM 4 para 76)

- Overhead rejoin (Entry 1): from the south along the inner base-leg line (3.22 NM along), at 3,500 ft and 220 KIAS. It must be established at least 1 NM before the pattern (WFO S2 art 401 para 7, SMM 4.15 para 35). The 10 Mile Lake and Farms references lie on this line about 2.6 and 5.6 NM south of the downwind (EFIG p.209, estimated from the map).
- Straight-in rejoin (Entry 2): from the south along the outer base-leg line (3.89 NM along), at 2,700 ft.
- Whiskey: a waypoint on the extended 29L downwind (SMM 4 para 76), about 5.1 NM west of the threshold, just past Race Track Lake (spot estimated from Fig 3-10).

## PFL (already built; moves with the true runway)

The circle is 0.5 NM in radius (now true NM; the old 3,038 ft was only 0.42 NM on the real ground). Its centre is 0.5 NM left of 29L at the threshold, so the circle closes on the centreline at the threshold (pfl-definition.md, ratified 4 Oct 07:03Z). High Key is over the threshold at 5,000 ft, Low Key at 3,700 ft and Final Key at 3,000 ft (SMM 13.8 para 17, 13.9 para 18). Pattern PFLs intercept the circle on a tangent (EFIG p.409): early in the pattern pointing at Low Key, abeam the runway between Low and Final Key, and past the runway just before Final Key.
