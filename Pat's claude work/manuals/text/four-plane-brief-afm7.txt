Take Off (2+2)

1.   Elements line up half/half.

2.   No. 3 ensures 500 ft separation from lead element and calls “IN”.

3.   Lead calls “RUNNING UP”, once complete No. 3 calls “READY”.

4.   Lead calls “ROLLING, ROLLING, NOW” . . . Brakes, Smash, Crack.

5.   No. 3 commands WTO on 5 sec delay with hand signals.

6.   After PTO, send elements to FW; 3/4 element rejoin to FW.

Interval Take-Off

For use in the event of crosswinds greater than 15 knots.

1.   Elements line up half/half.

2.   No. 3 ensures 500 ft separation from lead element and calls “IN”.

3.   Lead calls “RUNNING UP”, once complete No. 3 calls “READY”.

4.   Lead calls “ROLLING, ROLLING, NOW” . . . Brakes, PCL MAX.

     a.   5 sec: No. 2 brake release

     b.   10 sec: No. 3 brake release

     c.   15 sec: No 4. brake release

5. After PTO, separate elements rejoin to FW and then 3/4 element
rejoins to FW.

1.   Miss contract: 4 misses 3, misses 2, misses Lead

2.   Spacing: Work toward the near end of the range

3.   Sweep: Other than turns, ensure Lead can see you

1.   Start from wide LAB spacing (approx 6000 ft).

3.   Strive for accurate headings and G.

TRJ to FW from Spread 4

1. Lead will pause, allow No. 2 to establish closure, and then com-
mence a gentle turn towards No. 2.

2.   No. 2 conducts TRJ to FW spacing while maintaining stack.

3.   Nos. 3/4 conduct cold TRJs to FW spacing while maintaining stacks.

4. No. 3 must ensure positive separation from No. 2 (rejoining from
same distance, but hot for 2, cold for 3).

Fluid Manoeuvring

1. Lead will commence a 30 AOB turn in FW and ensure all aircraft call
ready. Once ready, Lead increase bank to 60 AOB and set PCL MAX for:

     a.   Exercise 1: 60/2 level turn

     b.   Exercise 3: Wingovers

     c.   Exercise 4: Barrel Roll (max 60 degrees of pitch permitted)

2.   Work towards 500’-750’ spacing during manoeuvres

3.   Terminate calls upon completion.

4. Lead will conduct gentle turn while re-establishing Finger geometry
at FW spacing.

Delays / Ground Abort

1.   Discuss

Take Off Abort

1.   Abort, Abort, Abort: All aircraft abort.

2. Aborting: All trailing aircraft abort. No. 3 must then call “3,4
ABORT, ABORT, ABORT”.

3.   Aborting aircraft must call “CLEAR” when able, from rear to front.

Lost Lead

1.   Responsibilities are, 4 misses 3, misses 2, misses Lead.

2.   If blind, avoid last known position and advise with altitude.

3.   Lead will coordinate deconfliction plan and BVR.

Collision

1.   Diverge/Decide/Discuss

Emergencies

1.   Non-critical emergencies: Advise and then Handle

2.   Critical emergencies: Handle and then Advise (promptly)

     a.   Close formation/GULAP: Ensure positive separation

3. Comm Fail: RTB OHB Stream unless signalled for early gear selec-
tion. If necessary lead may split formation to support emergency aircraft
in a single element.
Freeze Calls

1.   Use if uncertain during critical moments.

2.   Call “FREEZE”, with reason if able.

Training Rules




      25
Wx Limits: Discernible horizon — 5 NM flight vis, 5000’ between layers — 2000’
vertical / 1 NM horizontal
Altitude restrictions:
•     Hard deck 3000’ AGL or 2000’ above cloud
•     6000’ AGL with solo students
Manoeuvring Considerations:
•     Wingmen makes the miss (high if LL)
•     Lead remain predictable
•     Transmit your intended flight path
•     Head-on — Clear right
•     Nose high goes high if able
•     No crossing flight paths
•     Fluid maneouvring/high-aspect — 500’ bubble
•     LAB crossing — 300’ min safe separation
Flight Safety:
•     Wingmen losing visual or SA will manoeuvre away from lead’s last known
position and call BLIND with altitude
•     Lead losing sight or SA of wingmen will manoeuvre predictably
•     G-limits — Lead +4 G / Wingmen +5 G
•     Out of control / stall / spin recovery
•     Awareness of: into the sun / prop wash / fuel
•     Inadvertent cloud penetration
•     Ejection — OOC 6000’ AGL
•     Rescue cap responsibilities
Terminate: Ceases the current event or manoeuvre, is indicated by R/T or wing
rock, is acknowledged by all players in sequence and will be called when:
•     BINGO fuel is reached
•     Desired learning objectives are either met or unattainable
•     Breach of a training rule or other limits are recognized
•     Both lead and wingmen lose visual contact
•     Loss of comms with formation members
Knock-It-Off: Ceases all maneouvres, ends the overall mission, is indicated by R/T
or wing rock, is acknowledged by all players in sequence and will be called when:
•     Safety of flight is a factor
•     A dangerous situation develops (e.g. FM with 90 aspect, zero LOS, and high
closure)
•     Weather deteriorates below minimums
