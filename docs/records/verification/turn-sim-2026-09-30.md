# Turn Sim verification against the SMM (2026-09-30)

For Patrick. What the Formation Turn Sim flies for every formation, turn, direction and timing, compared with SMM chapter 16. The manual is cited by section and paragraph only.

- **LIVE** is what is on the site now (origin/main, commit 767aa5e).
- **NEXT** is the branch with the next batch of work (worktree-agent-a4910ee6636adb0d0, commit a9bbccc): clock cue, auto timing and compass heading.
- Each version was flown for 4 formations × 6 turns × 2 directions × 3 timings (144 runs each) at the screen defaults: 220 KTAS, 3 G, 6,000 ft spacing, 16 s base delay, offset box 7,000 ft aft, 75 s run. Turn degrees were set the way the screen sets them when you pick a turn: 90, 45, 90 (Hook), 45 (Shackle), 180 (Cross), 90 (In-place). Each run went until every aircraft had finished its turn, plus 10 s, or 75 s at most.
- Positions are given **relative to Lead at the end**: `R` is feet to Lead's right (a minus sign means left) and `F` is feet ahead of Lead's 3/9 line (a minus sign means behind). The formations start like this: two-ship #2 R6000. 4312: #2 R−6000, #3 R6000, #4 R12000. 2134 is the mirror image. Offset box: #2 R7000, #3 R3500 F−7000, #4 R10000 F−7000.
- The sim is flat (it has no altitude), so aircraft that cross in the sim are on top of each other. The SMM stacks them vertically in those turns.
- With **Time delay**, LIVE and NEXT fly exactly the same (112 of 144 runs are identical; only the start heading differs: LIVE starts east, NEXT starts north). They differ only in Clock and Auto timing on the delayed turns. On LIVE, Clock and Auto are greyed out on screen ("coming in a later step"), so a pilot can only fly Time delay there.

### Why "the shackle doesn't cross"

In the shackle, each wingman's turn is set to "the side it is on" (plan.js, shackle branch). That side is measured along V6's "right" vector, which is actually the aircraft's **left** on the map (the same mix-up D41 fixed for the Toward and Away settings). The result is that every wingman turns **away** from Lead. Lead always turns the way the Direction box says. In a two-ship:

- **Right:** Lead turns right and #2, on the right, turns right too. They fly parallel.
- **Left:** Lead turns left and #2 turns right. They split apart.

The rollback leg brings each aircraft back to its start heading. That is the only part that matches the SMM. The spec's line for V6 ("both aircraft turn the same way") is true only for Right in a two-ship.

### Why "the buttons don't do what they say"

- **Hook turn** is In-place 90 under another name: the same 90° and the same trail rollout in all 24 hook runs. If you type Turn degrees 180 by hand, Hook flies the SMM hook correctly in the 2-ship (#2 R−6000 F0) and in 4312 (R6000, R−6000, R−12000 LAB), but still with no rear-element delay in the offset box.
- **Cross turn** turns Lead and #2 the same way whenever the Direction box points away from #2 (Left in the two-ship and 2134, Right in 4312), because Lead always follows the Direction box.
- **Delayed 45** is the Delayed 90 plan cut to 45°. The wingman rolls out in trail, not in LAB.
