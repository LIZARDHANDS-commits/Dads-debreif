## 2. Contact Phase Aerobatics Syllabus (Clearhood Phase — SMM Ch 7)

### 7.4 The Aileron Roll
- **Entry Airspeed:** 230 KIAS.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Flight Path & Mechanics:**
  1. **Initiation:** Raise the nose smoothly to **20° nose-up** attitude on reference heading.
  2. **Roll Execution:** Relax elevator back pressure to achieve a 1.0 G ballistic arc, then apply coordinated aileron in the direction of roll. Maintain roll rate with aileron; slight barreling is natural and should not be countered with heavy forward stick.
  3. **Exit Gate:** Wings level on the original reference line at 230 KIAS (±10 kt).

### 7.5 The Loop (Dynamic G Pull Law)
- **Target Entry Airspeed:** **230 KIAS**.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Dynamic Pull Law (Patrick Operational Standard):**
  - The entry target is 230 KIAS.
  - If the aircraft enters **slower than 230 KIAS**, pull **more G** (up to 4.0 G / stick shaker limit) to reduce loop radius and ensure sufficient momentum across the top.
  - If the aircraft enters **faster than 230 KIAS**, pull **less G** (~3.0 G) to expand the loop and bleed excess energy.
- **Apex Criteria:** Cross the top inverted at 100–120 KIAS with slight positive G. The opposite horizon appears in the top of the canopy.
- **Exit Gate:** Modulate back pressure in the dive recovery to exit in level flight at exactly **230 KIAS**.

### 7.6 The Cuban Eight
- **Target Entry Airspeed:** **230 KIAS**.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Dynamic Pull Law:** Same as the loop: modulate initial G pull inversely with entry speed (more G if slow, less G if fast).
- **Inverted 45° Dive Line:** As the nose tracks down across the horizon, freeze pitch at 45° nose-down inverted. Execute a crisp 180° half-roll to upright at ~0.5 G around the 45° dive point.
- **Exit Gate:** Hold the 45° dive line accelerating back to **230 KIAS**, then pull into the second loop in the opposite direction, recovering level on original heading at **230 KIAS**.

### 7.7 The Cloverleaf
- **Target Entry Airspeed:** **230 KIAS**.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Dynamic Pull Law:** 2.5–3.5 G initial pull towards 60° pitch-up (more G if below 230 kt, less G if above).
- **Quarter Roll:** At 60° nose-up, blend coordinated rolling towards a 90° lateral ground reference point. Time the roll so the aircraft reaches the apex inverted, wings level, pointing directly at the 90° reference at 100–110 KIAS.
- **Exit Gate:** Pull through the bottom half of the loop, rolling out level at **230 KIAS** on the new heading.

### 7.8 The Hesitation Roll (Tactical Linking Maneuver)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Definition:** The Hesitation Roll is **NOT a civilian/competition 4-point roll.** In 15 Wing Moose Jaw training (SMM 7.8 paras 18–20), it is an aileron roll with a deliberate pause/hesitation **at the inverted position** designed to reset or build energy between maneuvers.

- **Purpose:** Tactical linking maneuver used to gain airspeed and set up follow-on vertical aerobatics (Loop, Cuban 8, Cloverleaf, Vertical 8).
- **Entry Airspeed:** Whatever airspeed is available exiting a previous maneuver (typically 180–230 KIAS).
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Flight Path & Mechanics (Patrick Operational Directive, D428):**
  1. **Initiation:** Pitch up smoothly to **20° nose-up** attitude on reference heading (identical initiation to the aileron roll).
  2. **Roll Inverted:** Roll smoothly inverted through 180° of roll.
  3. **The Hesitation (Setting the Dive Attitude):** At inverted, neutralize ailerons and **hesitate** (pause). Hold wings level inverted as the nose falls naturally through the horizon. The pilot uses this inverted pause to **SET the specific dive attitude required for the follow-on figure**:
     - *Shallower dive attitude* (~10°–15° nose-down) if accelerating to 230–250 KIAS (e.g., follow-on Loop, Cuban Eight, Cloverleaf, or Immelmann).
     - *Steeper dive attitude* (~25°–30° nose-down) if accelerating to 280 KIAS (e.g., follow-on Vertical Eight or Vertical Roll).
  4. **The Rollout:** Once the target dive attitude is established on the horizon reference, roll smoothly upright back to wings-level in the established dive.
  5. **The Acceleration:** Accelerate along the set dive line directly to the target entry airspeed for the next figure, pulling up into the figure as target entry speed is reached.

### 7.9 Roll Off the Top (Basic Immelmann)
- **Entry Airspeed:** **Any speed above 220 KIAS** (nominal 250 KIAS in SMM Table 7.1).
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Flight Path:** Pull 3.5–4.0 G into a vertical half-loop.
- **Rollout Point:** At 10–20° above the horizon inverted, freeze elevator pitch and execute a rapid, coordinated 180° half-roll to upright at ~0.5 G.
- **Exit Gate:** Level flight upright at 120–140 KIAS on reciprocal heading (+180°), with a gain of ~2,500–3,000 ft MSL.

### 7.10 Half Roll Pull Through (Basic Split-S)
- **Entry Airspeed:** 120–140 KIAS (academic clearhood).
- **Power:** IDLE (avoids excessive dive speed).
- **Flight Path:** Raise nose ~20°, roll inverted at 0.5 G. Check wings level inverted, then smoothly pull through the vertical at 3.0–4.0 G.
- **Exit Gate:** Roll out level at 230 KIAS on reciprocal heading (+180°), losing ~2,000–2,500 ft.

## 3. Advanced Aerobatics & Energy Management (SMM Ch 14)

### 14.14 Level & Constant-Speed Max-Performance Turns (MPT)
- **Academic Formula:** Optimal entry airspeed is $150\text{ KIAS} - (\text{Altitude in Thousands})$ (e.g. 140 KIAS at 10,000 ft MSL).
- **Optimum Rate:** 160 KIAS at 17 units AOA (SMM 14.13 para 31).
- **Bank Angle:** SMM prescribes ~75° visual attitude cue; aircraft sustained turn charts establish **69° aerodynamic bank** at 2.8 G sustained at 10,000 ft (D112).
- **Tolerance:** 160 KIAS held within $\pm 5\text{ kt}$ (SMM 14.14).

### 14.15 Tactical Immelmann (BFM)
- **Airspeed Envelope:** **Any speed above 220 KIAS** (up to 316 KIAS VMO).
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Pull Law:** Pull ~4.0 G smoothly into the stick shaker (15.5 units AOA).
- **Rollout:** Initiate roll upright 25° above horizon on the backside. Rollout airspeed is slow (~113–120 KIAS), immediately handing over to MPT acceleration.

### 14.16 Tactical Split-S (BFM)
- **Airspeed Envelope:** 100–120 KIAS.
- **Power:** MAX (tactical BFM energy retention).
- **Pull Law:** Raise nose ~20°, roll inverted at 0.5 G, pull through in stick shaker capped at **5.0 G** (ratified by Patrick, D407).
- **Altitude Loss:** Exactly 1,688 ft from entry altitude (1,976 ft from apex), matching the SMM ~2,000 ft rule of thumb. Minimum entry altitude: 10,000 ft MSL (SMM 14.16 para 40).

### 14.17 Pitch Back (Climbing BFM Reversal)
- **Airspeed Envelope:** 160–220 KIAS.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Bank Angle:** Scaled continuously from **60° bank at 160 KIAS** down to **30° bank at 220 KIAS** (EFIG p.441).
- **Asymmetric Limit Guard:** Above 190 KIAS, G is strictly capped at **4.7 G** to prevent structural over-G in rolling maneuvers. Turn completes under 180° heading change before MPT transition.

### 14.18 Slice Turn (Descending BFM Reversal)
- **Airspeed Envelope:** 100–160 KIAS.
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Bank Angle:** Scaled from **90° bank at 160 KIAS** up to **135° bank (inverted slice) at 100 KIAS** (SMM 14.18 para 46).
- **Safety Clamp:** Hard Deck floor clamp (6,000 ft MSL). At $\le 140\text{ KIAS}$, if altitude $< \text{Hard Deck} + 2,000\text{ ft}$, aircraft must fly a slice turn, never a Split-S.

### 14.19 Vertical Eight & Vertical Roll
- **Entry Airspeed:** **280 KIAS** (SMM Table 14.1).
- **Power:** MAX.
- **Pull Law:** 4.0–5.0 G initial vertical pull.
- **Aero Reality:** Entering below 260 KIAS lacks sufficient specific kinetic energy ($V^2/2g$) to complete two consecutive vertical loops in the CT-156 without stalling at the second apex.

## 4. Authentic SMM Aerobatics Sequences (FF48)

The SMM Aerobatics Sequence Mode utilizes authentic Canadian military training sequences, linking energy-building maneuvers with vertical figures:

### Sequence 1: Classic Contact Flow (Clearhood Phase II)
1. **Takeoff & Climb:** Climb to 10,000 ft MSL, trim at 230 KIAS.
2. **The Loop:** 230 KIAS entry, dynamic G pull, float inverted 110 KIAS, exit at **230 KIAS**.
3. **The Cuban Eight:** 230 KIAS entry, half-roll on 45° dive lines, exit at **230 KIAS**.
4. **Hesitation Roll (Airspeed Reset):** Pitch up 20°, roll inverted, pause as nose drops through horizon to establish calibrated dive attitude (~15° dive), roll upright, accelerate to **230 KIAS** entry into Cloverleaf.
5. **The Cloverleaf:** 230 KIAS entry, 2.5–3.5 G pull, 90° roll at apex, exit at **230 KIAS** (90° heading change).
6. **Roll Off the Top (Immelmann):** Any speed $>220\text{ KIAS}$ (e.g. 240 KIAS), pull 4.0 G, roll upright at +15°, exit 130 KIAS on 180° heading.
7. **Half Roll Pull Through (Split-S):** 130 KIAS entry, IDLE power, 0.5 G roll, 3.5 G pull-out back to 230 KIAS.

### Sequence 2: Tactical Energy & BFM Flow (Phase III)
1. **Hesitation Roll (High-Energy Dive Reset):** Pitch up 20°, roll inverted, pause as nose drops into steep calibrated dive attitude (~30° dive), roll upright, accelerate to **280 KIAS** entry into Vertical Eight.
2. **Vertical Eight:** 280 KIAS entry, 4.5 G pull into upper loop, lower half-loop, exit 280 KIAS.
3. **Pitch Back:** 210 KIAS, 35° bank, 4.0 G climbing reversal, hand to MPT at 160 KIAS.
4. **Level MPT:** Sustained 69° bank, 160 KIAS, 17 units AOA for 360° turn.
5. **Slice Turn:** 140 KIAS, 110° bank descending reversal, recover to level flight.
6. **Tactical Split-S:** 115 KIAS entry, MAX power, 5.0 G pull in shaker, lose 1,700 ft, exit 220 KIAS.

## 5. Pilot Domain Tolerances & Invariant Rules (D369, D371, D411)

- **Airspeed:** $\pm 10\text{ kt}$ standard, $\pm 20\text{ kt}$ tactical.
- **Load Factor:** $\pm 0.5\text{ G}$ standard, $\pm 1.0\text{ G}$ tactical.
- **Bank Angle:** $\pm 5^\circ$ standard, $\pm 10^\circ$ tactical.
- **Altitude Separation:** $\pm 100\text{ ft}$ standard, $\pm 200\text{ ft}$ tactical.
- **Hard Deck Safety Floor:** 6,000 ft MSL (3,000 ft AGL at Moose Jaw). Simulation forcibly levels off or halts if aircraft breaches 6,000 ft MSL.
- **Stall Speed Baseline ($V_s$):** 86 KIAS at 1.0 G clean. Stick shaker onset at $15.5\text{ units AOA}$ (5–10 kt above stall).
- **Asymmetric Rolling G Limit:** 4.7 G max whenever roll rate exceeds $15^\circ/\text{s}$.
