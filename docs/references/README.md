# References

Notes that point to manual pages. Page references only: manual text and images never go here, and the CFAFM is never cited by content. The manuals themselves are private, in the project files.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `smm-aerobatics-catalog.md` | How each aerobatic manoeuvre is flown, by SMM page, with Patrick's rulings. The default reference, not a wall. |
| `traffic-pattern-matrix.md` | The Moose Jaw circuit waypoints the Traffic sim flies, with their sources. |
| `afman11-202v3-alternates.md` | When a USAF base needs an alternate and what makes one suitable (AFMAN 11-202 Vol 3, Chapter 4), in our own words with paragraph numbers. For the SOF's US bases. |
| `aetcman11-248-cockpit.md` | The T-6 cockpit and formation sight pictures in AETCMAN 11-248 (13 Aug 2025) by figure and page, in our own words, with what the Formation Sim's CT-156 cockpit and model do against each (TS-156). |
| `open-source-performance-baseline.md` | Public civil TCDS, manufacturer technical sheets, DTIC/NASA papers, and JSBSim flight dynamics models justifying airframe, engine, V-speeds, and G-limits without reference to restricted military manuals. |

Other reading notes on the manuals (traffic pattern numbers, formation and turn numbers, weather limits, the energy model check) stay with the manuals in the project files, because they quote manual figures.
