# References

Notes that point to manual pages, and extracts from them (text and figures), each with its page. The CFAFM never goes here (ALL-41). The manuals themselves are kept in the project files.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `smm-aerobatics-catalog.md` | How each aerobatic manoeuvre is flown, by SMM page, with Patrick's rulings. The default reference, not a wall. |
| `traffic-pattern-matrix.md` | The Moose Jaw circuit waypoints the Traffic sim flies, with their sources. |
| `afman11-202v3-alternates.md` | When a USAF base needs an alternate and what makes one suitable (AFMAN 11-202 Vol 3, Chapter 4), in our own words with paragraph numbers. For the SOF's US bases. |
| `aetcman11-248-cockpit.md` | The T-6 cockpit and formation sight pictures in AETCMAN 11-248 (13 Aug 2025) by figure and page, in our own words, with what the Formation Sim's CT-156 cockpit and model do against each (TS-156). |
| `smm-fighting-wing-and-pursuit.md` | Everything the SMM, EFIG and Gen Book say about fighting wing, pursuit curves and the formation visual cues, by reference, with the gaps and conflicts against Turn Sim spec 7e (14 Oct 2026). |
| `figures/smm-12-29-*`, `figures/smm-12-30-*` | SMM 12.29-12.30, fighting wing and the pursuit curves (paras 68-74, Figs 12.19-12.25), Patrick's page captures (14 Oct 2026); used by Turn Sim spec 7e. |
| `formation-control-research-14oct.md` | Web research for the Formation Sim's fighting wing technique (B5, TS-233): formation control behind a turning Lead, collision-avoidance safety filters, pursuit angle as range control, the vertical as an energy trade, pilot delay models, and public non-RCAF trainer formation manuals (US Navy T-34C cruise, a civil T-6 SOP). Background only, under the SMM and Patrick. |
| `open-source-performance-baseline.md` | Public civil TCDS, manufacturer technical sheets, DTIC/NASA papers, and JSBSim flight dynamics models justifying airframe, engine, V-speeds, and G-limits from public sources and pilots' experience. |

Other reading notes on the manuals (traffic pattern numbers, formation and turn numbers, weather limits, the energy model check) stay with the manuals in the project files, because they quote manual figures.
