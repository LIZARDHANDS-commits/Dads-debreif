# Traffic Pattern Sim requirements

The whole-tool requirements in `../../REQUIREMENTS.md` apply too. How each line is checked is in `testing.md`.

Sources in this file point to where things were on 4 Oct 2026: `pf/` means the project files (`pf/`, private), and repo paths such as `docs/records/` or `specs/` are now under `archive/` (see `archive/README.md`).


**State today.**
- The new app (`#/traffic`) flies a wind-aware three-mode aircraft engine, with a 2D map, a 3D view, PFL/High Key/Breakout/Closed-pattern/Go-around buttons, and a wide settings file. Several things Patrick asked for are missing or contradicted by the shipped screen: the left side no longer defines patterns (the route editor was removed in commit 3ced22f), crab angle and ground speed are not shown anywhere, there is no way to remove one aircraft, "traffic on final" avoidance is not built (the screen says so), and the default scenario still opens with V6-style names and a 15 minute spawn schedule.
- A lot was built that Patrick's own 30 Sep "Streamlined" words put on the future list (PFLs, engine-outs, 3D polish). Later log rows say Patrick ratified parts of it in "/grill-me" sessions. That is a real conflict between sources; it is recorded, not resolved (sections 5 and 7).

## Realistic kinematics means (Traffic)

Each aircraft is a real aircraft in moving air. It flies at its own indicated airspeed (converted to true airspeed for its height), banks at a realistic roll rate, turns at the rate its bank and speed give, and the wind moves the air under it every step, the same way in every manoeuvre, in calm air or 30 kt. Routine legs follow planned, wind-shaped paths and manoeuvres are flown under live flight control (TR-R30); either way, what you see must be what a real aircraft would do. The picture the manuals set: SMM 4.14 paras 32-33 (220 KIAS pattern, 60 degrees of bank, circuit checks); SMM 4.17 paras 38-42 and EFIG p.151, p.183 (break: level 180 degree turn at 60 degrees and 2 G, idle power, about 2,000 ft past the threshold in a 10 kt headwind, later for more wind; downwind below the gear limit and about 120 KIAS); SMM 4.19 paras 43-48 and EFIG p.150-152 (final turn from the perch, up to 45 degrees of bank, 120 KIAS, 110 minimum); SMM 4.7 paras 10-12 (3 degree path, window at 3/4 NM); SMM 4.1 para 1 and Table 4.1 (threshold crossing speed); SMM 4.5 para 8 and 4.6 para 9 (straight-in base height and speeds); SMM 4.9 para 15 (touchdown speed); EFIG p.134 (closed pattern); and for forced landings the T-6A max glide chart, SMM 13.5 paras 7-8, 13.8 para 17, NFM Fig 3-4 and 3-5 pp.3-12 and 3-13 (125 KIAS clean glide, about 2 NM per 1,000 ft, zoom, High Key 5,000 ft MSL at Moose Jaw). Patrick's own overrides win over the manuals (3,500 ft pattern, D109; 100 KIAS from window to threshold, D110). What is NOT a requirement: the D371 pilot tolerances (plus or minus 10 kt, 100 ft, 5 degrees, 0.5 G), any lap, break or landing time, V6's numbers (V6 has no wind at all and flies a dot along a polyline), the bit-exact break-decel formula, the 29.8 s perch shift figure, or any pass or fail based on how long a leg takes. Because wind and physics changes move all times, tests should check shape and invariants (speed only falls in the break, height flat, bank under the limit, rolls out on the centreline, same setup gives the same picture).
