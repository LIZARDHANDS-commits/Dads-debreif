# Turn Fight requirements

The whole-tool requirements in `../../REQUIREMENTS.md` apply too. How each line is checked is in `testing.md`.

Sources in this file point to where things were on 4 Oct 2026: `pf/` means the project files (`pf/`, private), and repo paths such as `docs/records/` or `specs/` are now under `archive/` (see `archive/README.md`).


**State today.** V6's Turn Fight was a small viewer: two jets at constant speed and G, head-on, 1-circle or 2-circle, who gets the nose on first (`original/shell.html:778`, `original/shell.html:4232-4294`). The approved spec kept that and added Start geometry (R28), an optional Energy mode and a 2D/3D switch (`specs/SPEC-turn-fight.md:9-19`). The build on main (v2.5) has gone much further: it opens straight into Energy mode with a tactical-AI pilot, gun-kill auto-pause, mid-air collision and tumble, yo-yos and five preset engagements (`src/modules/turn-fight/state.js:155`, `docs/handover/turn-fight.md:35-50`). Many of those were never in a requirement or the approved spec (see scope-and-ideas.md), and several records contradict each other (see questions.md).

## Realistic kinematics means (Turn Fight)

Each T-6 is a point that flies through the air at a speed, heading, bank and climb angle a pilot could fly, and a coordinated turn means bank and speed set rate and radius (radius = V squared over g times the square root of G squared minus one). Energy is traded honestly: pulling to the stall line costs the turn, climbing bleeds speed, diving gains it, full power adds a little, and no jet exceeds its V-n limits (+7 G, +4.7 G rolling, the stall line near (KIAS/86) squared G, VMO 316 KIAS, Mach 0.67) without the screen flagging it. The pictures come from the Moose Jaw manuals by page: coordinated-turn and V-n numbers (`pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:55-75`), the max-performance turns and entry speeds (SMM 14.3 para 6, 14.4 para 8, 14.14 paras 32-38, Table 14.1, 14.15-14.18; EFIG p.428, 430, 435, 438, 441; NFM p.1-52, 6-6, 6-7), the start terms (SMM 12.2 paras 5-9) and the hard deck (SMM 14.6 para 16). Where the manuals give no number (stall speed 86 kt, 94 % shaker, roll rate, mid throttle) the model keeps a labelled working value for Dad to check (`specs/SPEC-turn-fight.md:323-339`). What is NOT a requirement: matching V6's numbers (1,106 ft, 19.2 deg/s, "+18.2 s"), landing inside the D371 box, hitting a nose-on at a given second, or the 10-minute figure as a test gate. The simple model (constant speed and G) is a geometry teaching aid and says so on screen; its 4 G or 5 G is not claimed to be sustainable (`pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:75`).
