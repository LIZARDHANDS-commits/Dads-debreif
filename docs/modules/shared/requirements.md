# Shared parts requirements

The shared parts (flight core, app frame and home screen, weather, airfields, storage, the screen kit) have no requirements of their own: they serve the whole-tool requirements in `../../REQUIREMENTS.md` (ALL-R1 to ALL-R28, ratified by Patrick on 4 Oct 2026) and the module requirements that use them. The flying lines (ALL-R19 to ALL-R24, ALL-R28) are the ones the flight core answers to.
