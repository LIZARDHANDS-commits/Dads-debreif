The whole-tool requirements in `../../REQUIREMENTS.md` apply too. How each line is checked is in `testing.md`.

Sources in this file point to where things were on 4 Oct 2026: `pf/` means the project files (`pf/`, private), and repo paths such as `docs/records/` or `specs/` are now under `archive/` (see `archive/README.md`).


**State today.** The Turn Sim on `main` (commit 6283f38) opens cleanly and flies the SMM turns. It has three columns, a closed settings menu and a Layers menu, and no browser errors in the agent's runs. But several things the plan says are built are not: the Profiles panel is a placeholder with one sentence in it (`src/modules/turn-sim/layout.js:198-201`), there is no Export CSV, no spacing graph, no solver button, no wingman dragging and no G-warm panel (all engine-only or on paused branches). Two things in the pictures do look wrong to a pilot and need Patrick or Dad: the "4312" preset is drawn left to right as #2 #1 #3 #4 (V6 does the same; TS-Q5), and the default Timing is a fixed 16 s delay, which conflicts with Patrick's D380 "wingmen turn when it makes the spacing work" (TS-Q4).

## Realistic kinematics means (Turn Sim)

Each aircraft flies a constant-speed coordinated level turn at the set G, so radius, turn rate and bank follow from speed and G (today, at 220 kt flown as ground speed and 3 G: radius about 1,515 ft; with 220 KIAS converted to true airspeed at 8,000 ft the radius is about a quarter larger, TS-Q18; about 14 degrees per second, about 70 degrees of bank; `src/core/flight-math.js:29-52`, screen `agents/shots/turn-sim/new-default.png`). Constant speed matches the SMM's own wording for these turns (a 3 G, energy-sustaining level turn with power as required: SMM 16.18 para 50, 16.19 para 52, `pf/manuals/text/smm.txt:8217,8239`), though the T-6 charts say sustained 3 G is about the limit near sea level and sustained turn disappears by about 260 KIAS (`pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:73`). The SMM manuals set the picture: SMM chapter 16 (16.13, 16.18-16.22, 16.41-16.45; figures 16.15 to 16.21 and 16.30 to 16.36 listed at `pf/manuals/README.md:49-68`), and chapter 12 for rejoins and fighting wing (future). Who turns when must follow what a pilot does: outside aircraft first, inside aircraft on the 5 or 7 o'clock cue, and the end picture of every turn must match the figure. What the engine does **not** model, and what I could not find a requirement for: roll-in time (bank is instant, `src/modules/turn-sim/engine/step.js:89-100`), speed loss in a turn, altitude, and wind (TS-Q6-Q8). What is **not** a requirement: the D371 tolerances (Patrick 3 Oct, `pf/reset/consolidation-plan.md:9-12`), any time such as "16.16 s" or "36.7 s" as a pass/fail number (the current test pins one: `tests/unit/turn-sim/auto-timing.test.js:63`), and V6's numbers, including V6's 25.4 s auto step, which Dad already called wrong (D44, `archive/docs/records/plan-decisions.md:417`).
