# Turn Sim: future ideas

Ideas for the Turn Sim that are not being built. Nothing here is started until the Turn Sim review in `plan.md` Step 1 has decided how the module is built. An idea moves into `plan.md` only with Patrick's yes (TQ-1), and a new idea goes on this list the same day it is asked for. "Feature Ideas" numbers are from the researched list of 54 ideas, kept at https://claude.ai/artifact/6PMvFiKigB29hBvVSoJ2op (`pf/reset/2-inventory/agents/sources/feature-ideas.md:30`). "FF" numbers are from the old plan's future-features list (`docs/records/future-ideas.md:5`).

The second wave of live mode (rejoins, fighting wing and fluid manoeuvring, old FF39 to FF41) is not listed here: Patrick put it in the plan, in the order the review sets, so its single home is `plan.md` Step 4 (`pf/reset/1-requirements/questions.md:165`). The G-warm exercise (two-ship and four-ship) is also in the plan: its question is TS-Q19 and the four-ship picture check is a Step 1 item (`pf/reset/1-requirements/questions.md:164`).
