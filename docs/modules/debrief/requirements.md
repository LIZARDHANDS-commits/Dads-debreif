## Flight math in the replay means

The replay is the aircraft's own track, so it must show what a T-6 can actually do and say plainly when the track cannot tell us. Positions come from the recorded fixes; speed, heading and climb from the track around the moment; G, bank and pitch are estimates from that motion and are labelled as estimates (Patrick's Q32 answer, `archive/specs/SPEC-flight-data.md:52`). A level turn at 60° of bank is 2 G, and bank and G in a steady level turn must agree (EFIG p.183, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:59`). A G the aircraft cannot pull at its speed (the sustained limit follows the stall line, about (KIAS ÷ 86)², and 7 G symmetric, V-n chart, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:71`) is a GPS glitch and shows as "--". Formation judgements use the SMM's picture: spread 4,000 to 6,000 ft with 0 to 10° of sweep (SMM 16.18 para 49), the offset box 6,000 to 8,000 ft back (SMM 16.41 para 109), Lead 220 KIAS in the Low block and 200 in the Mid block (Gen Book p.12), 300 ft minimum crossing separation (SMM 16.13 para 31) and the 500 ft bubble (Gen Book p.11) (`pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:9`, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:10`, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:18`, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:25`, `pf/manuals/formation-and-turn-numbers.md:51`). Airspeed against those targets is KIAS, which needs the air's motion, so wind must enter the same way for every ship (DB-R8). A GPS ground track in wind is not the air track: a steady turn looks faster on the downwind side and slower upwind, so ground-track G and turn rate swing unless wind is taken out. G here is the level-turn estimate from heading change; a pull in the vertical plane (loop, pull-up) reads near 1 G because it does not look at climb-angle change (`src/flight-data/flight.js:206-229`; the 3D view's "wings-level pulls" bug is #14, `docs/references/v6-audit/findings.json:4103-4118`). What is NOT a requirement: the pilot tolerances D371 (±10 kt, ±100 ft, ±5°, ±0.5 G, ±0.5 s) (`archive/docs/REMEDIATION_ROADMAP.md:40-46`), matching V6's numbers to the decimal (the spec still says "The numbers stay V6's (D29)", `archive/specs/SPEC-debrief.md:134`, though D29 is marked reversed, `archive/docs/records/plan-decisions.md:38`), and any pass or fail on a clock time such as "start+1688" (the current readout tests pin example-flight seconds, `tests/unit/debrief/readouts.test.js:307-321`).
