## 3D cockpit view and smooth GPS gap fill (Dad, 10 Oct 2026)

Dad's ask, in his words: "add 3D from the cockpit to the KML viewer ... I want the tracks the pilots upload, if there is a GPS gap, for the web tool to auto solve where they might be based on others and previous data then next position data. So if a 90 degree turn is missing the middle ... then there must be a smooth bank in there."

1. **Cockpit view in the Debrief's 3D:** sit in any loaded ship's front (or rear) seat and replay the sortie from there, reusing the shared CT-156 cockpit (`src/ui-kit/ct156-cockpit.js`, TS-154 to TS-157, PR #697) with the ship's attitude worked out from its track.
2. **Smooth gap fill:** where a track has a gap (`src/flight-data/clean.js` GAP_S, 5 s), estimate the missing path from the fixes before and after (position, speed, heading, height and their rates) and, in a formation, from the other ships' tracks over the same seconds, so a turn missing its middle is drawn as a smooth banked turn the aircraft could fly, not a straight chord. Before V2.211 the Debrief broke the line at a gap (no straight line is drawn) and slid a hollow marker along the straight chord between the fixes, marked as a guess (`inGap`, C4); with the fill off it still does.

Moved up to plan.md Step 3a (Patrick approved, relayed by Dad, 10 Oct 2026). Filled stretches must stay marked as estimates on screen and never feed the Debrief's verdicts as if they were real fixes, unless Patrick says otherwise. A design proposal is being written for him first.
