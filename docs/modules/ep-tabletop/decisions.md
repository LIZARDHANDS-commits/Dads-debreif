| EP-2 | **The instructor's controls run on the instructor's phone, opened by a QR code; the student's screen never shows the setup, the developments to come, the end result or the changes being made** (Dad: "the IP would QR code with their phone to hide the setup, so the student didn't know the end results ... same with the pauses"). One-screen mode behind an Instructor switch stays as the fallback. How the link is made waits on EP-Q1. | spec | Dad, 12 Oct 2026. Wording approved by Patrick (relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026). |
| EP-3 | **No boldface, checklist or performance-chart text is shown, stored or shipped.** The student says the steps; the instructor marks them against their own checklist. | spec, testing | Rule book (controlled content). Wording approved by Patrick (relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026). |
| EP-4 | **The cockpit's engine and systems displays, the caution panel and the three lip lamps are drawn live from the scenario** through an additive `liveEngine` option in the shared cockpit (other sims don't pass it and draw exactly as before). An indication the student hasn't asked about shows as the static panel does (needle at the scale's start, dashes, the light dark), so nothing shows before it is revealed. The flight instruments (attitude, heading, altitude, airspeed) always show: the student flies by them in Aircraft control. Gear, flaps and the outside cues (smoke, flames, oil on the windscreen, roughness, noises) show as words in the student's Revealed list; the panel's gear lights stay static in Step 1. The view starts looking along the nose 12° down, so the panel and the horizon share the picture (an estimate). | `src/ui-kit/ct156-cockpit.js`, `src/modules/ep-tabletop/` | Build choice, Step 1 (12 Oct 2026). Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-5 | **Going to Analyse stops time and going to Boldface restarts it** (para 1.20.3.2.1, 1.20.3.3); the instructor can pause or resume at any time, and a session waits ("Waiting for the instructor") until the instructor presses Start. | `src/modules/ep-tabletop/scenario.js` | Build choice, Step 1. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-6 | **The console's development controls:** Now fires the next development; Later moves it 60 s on (a timed one) or one step on (a step one); + Add makes the changes not yet applied into the next development (on tap, in 1 min, or at a step) instead of applying them; Undo drops the changes not yet applied. "Apply each change at once" is a tick box, off by default. | `src/modules/ep-tabletop/scenario.js`, `console.js` | Build choice, Step 1. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-7 | **The Moose Jaw 3D environment is built from the shared pieces** (real terrain, the satellite picture draped on it, Patrick's CYMJ runways and the fields round it, the shared sky, a haze to the horizon), centred on CYMJ. Traffic's richer CYMJ world (buildings, landmarks, runway paint) stays in Traffic for now: it can't be moved as plain file moves (see the proposal in the README). Headings in the scenario are magnetic; the world is drawn true with 9° E (TR-65), a constant the module keeps beside Traffic's own copy until a shared home is agreed. | `src/modules/ep-tabletop/world3d.js` | Patrick, relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026 ("use the moose jaw 3d environment"); how it is built is a build choice. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
