| EP-8 | **The phone link is the relay mailbox (EP-Q1 option (b)) first.** The phone PUTs the student view to our Netlify relay's `/ep-link` at most twice a second (the latest winning, and again every 4 s so the screen knows it is there); the student's screen GETs it about once a second, and with nothing fresher than 10 s it says "Instructor link lost — waiting" and keeps the last state (EP-R10). The direct link (option (a), WebRTC) waits: it needs a public STUN server, a new outside service (EP-Q4). The link sits behind one small interface (`createLink` in `link.js`), so (a) can be added beside it. The QR code opens `#/ep-tabletop?console&s=<session>&k=<key>` (the shell's router reads only the part before "?", and a route with a sub-path such as `/console` is "not found" by its tested rule). The relay address is the shared one (`src/wx/relay.js`), kept in the module's own `relay` setting. | `src/modules/ep-tabletop/link.js`, `index.js`, `src/wx/relay.js` | Patrick approved Step 2 and option (b), relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026. How it is built is a build choice. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-9 | **The phone is the master and sends only the student view.** The scenario engine runs on the phone. One pure function, `studentView(session)`, makes the student's side: the situation given at the start, the step and steps visited, time running or still and the clock, the FEVER and TCCC prompts, and the panel's settings for the flight instruments and what has been revealed. It never holds the scenario's name or script, the developments to come, edits not yet applied, marks, notes, the Declare answers, the log or how it ends (EP-2). One-screen mode draws from the same function. The student's screen treats what arrives as untrusted: `cleanStudentView` keeps only known fields and indications, clamps numbers to their gauges' ranges and words to short plain text, and draws them as text. | `src/modules/ep-tabletop/student-view.js`, `index.js` | Build choice, Step 2 (12 Oct 2026), from Dad's EP-2 and the rule book's untrusted-input line. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-10 | **The relay keeps each session in Netlify Blobs for at most 6 hours.** `/ep-link` stores { key hash, created, last write, student view }: the session id and key are each 128 random bits (base64url, checked by strict pattern), the first PUT keeps a SHA-256 hash of the key and a later PUT with another key is refused, a view is a JSON object of at most 16 KB, DELETE with the key ends it, and an entry older than 6 hours from its first PUT reads as gone and is deleted when touched (a new session also sweeps old ones). The same allowed origins as every relay answer, never "allow all"; nothing about who asked is logged. `relay/package.json` holds the one package, `@netlify/blobs` 11.1.1. | `relay/ep-link.js`, `relay/netlify/functions/ep-link.mjs`, `relay/package.json` | Netlify Blobs: Patrick's yes, relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026. The 6-hour expiry and 16 KB cap are build choices. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick. |
| EP-11 | **Faults drive the gauges; the gauges drive the lights.** The instructor sets causes (oil leak, chip, fuel imbalance, fire, generator failure, roughness, engine failure, hydraulic or fuel leak) with a severity and a trend; the gauges follow from the causes and the clock; each caution or warning light comes on from a threshold on its gauge (MASTER CAUTION with any caution, MASTER WARN with FIRE). The instructor's typed values and light ticks still win. Thresholds and fault rates without a manual page or Patrick's ruling are estimates, shown "(estimate)" on the console; lights are references, never walls. | `systems.js` (Step 3) | Dad, 12 Oct 2026 ("certain pressures should trigger a light"); `systems-plan.md` section 2. Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick; Patrick said yes to building the plan (relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026). |
| EP-12 | **Visual cues stay light:** cue words in the Revealed list, a light that flickers by the clock, a small cockpit shake for roughness, smoke haze and fire glow as overlays on the student's screen. Each reaches the student only once the instructor applies it. No 3D cues while the module is being built (future list). | Step 4 | Dad, 12 Oct 2026; rule book (3D is a bonus). Wording not yet confirmed with Patrick; Patrick said yes to building the plan (relayed by Dad, 12 Oct 2026). |
