How it works: one "site profile" per home base in `src/modules/sof/sites/` (`index.js` with `siteFor(icao)` and a `GENERIC` profile for any other field, then `cymj.js`, `kdlf.js` …). A profile holds the SOF's own data for that base: airspace (today's entry shape, each with its source), watched areas, routes, towns, anchor stations, tour targets, magnetic variation with its source, which radar, lightning, satellite, model and NOTAM services to use (with credits), the map box, charts yes or no, and `standards` (home trigger, alternate rules, crosswind levels, lightning radius, each with a page) or `null`. The home field stays the shared airfields setting; a **Home base** choice in SOF settings sets home and the base's usual alternates through it. A source that is `null` draws nothing and says "can't tell", never a tick (ECCC pictures come back empty outside Canada and must never read as "no lightning").
