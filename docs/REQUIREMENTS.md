## "Realistic kinematics means" (whole tool)
Aircraft move the way real T-6s fly. Each one flies through the air at a speed, heading, bank and climb or descent that a pilot could fly, and the wind carries the air. Turns are coordinated, so bank and speed set the turn rate and radius. Energy is traded honestly. The pictures come from the Moose Jaw manuals, cited by page, and standard aerodynamics. What is **not** a requirement: matching V6's numbers, landing inside a ±10 kt or ±100 ft box, or reaching a point at a particular second. Checks look at what must always be true (invariants) and what happened (it landed on the runway, it rolled out in trail), not at exact numbers or times.
